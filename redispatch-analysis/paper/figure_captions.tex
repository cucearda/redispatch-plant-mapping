% Funnel: published records -> analysis dataset

\caption{From published redispatch records to the analysis dataset.
Successive filtering steps applied to the raw \"UNB redispatch events: from
123{,}362 published events we retain those joined to a plant-matcher row
(105{,}668), those with resolved coordinates (101{,}793), those matched with
confidence above 0.5 (86{,}561), and finally those whose measure reason
(\texttt{GRUND\_DER\_MASSNAHME}) is load-induced (83{,}716). Bar labels give the
surviving event count and, in parentheses, the number dropped at each step.
About 32\% of published events are removed, leaving 83{,}716 confidently
located, load-induced events for analysis.}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
% Redispatch decomposition, indexed to 2013
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
\caption{Decomposition of total redispatch energy, indexed to 2013
(base $=1.0$). Total energy (purple) is factored into the number of events $N$
(green), the mean duration per event $\bar d$ (blue), and the mean power per
event $\bar P$ (orange), so that $E = N\cdot\bar d\cdot\bar P$. By 2024 total
redispatch energy has risen roughly sevenfold, growth driven almost entirely by
the number of events; mean duration less than doubles and plateaus after 2022,
while mean power per event stays flat and slightly declines. The dashed line
marks the Redispatch~2.0 / NABEG transition (Oct 2021), after which the rise in
event count steepens.}

\caption{Battery siting-value map: grid cells coloured by local siting value
$dV$ (M\euro/year per 50~MW) from the redispatch wedge, over 910 candidate
cells (darker $=$ higher; colour scale normalised at the 98th percentile,
14.59~M\euro/year; max 15.79~M\euro/year). Value concentrates in eastern
Germany, where all top-10 sites (circles) fall around Saxony-Anhalt and Saxony,
tracing the congested corridors where redispatch interventions concentrate. The
380/220~kV transmission grid is overlaid for reference.}

\caption{Unmatchable redispatch volume (market-based \emph{B\"orse} and
countertrade measures) by instructing TSO (\texttt{ANWEISENDER\_UENB}), as a
share of the total 182.0~TWh. These measures make up 15.7\% of all volume and
are dominated by TenneT~DE (13.7\%, mostly countertrade); TransnetBW (1.0\%),
Amprion (0.6\%), and 50Hertz (0.4\%) contribute little. This volume is excluded
from the location-based analysis, as no physical bottleneck can be attributed to
it.}

% Match rate by TSO under two definitions
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
\caption{Match rate by instructing TSO under two definitions of ``matched,''
each measured by share of events, of distinct plants, and of energy volume
(MWh). GEO (left) counts any plant match with usable coordinates; Data used
(right) applies the strict analysis criterion (confidence $>0.5$ and a valid
measure reason). TransnetBW is best covered under both definitions (91\%
events / 93\% MWh under GEO; 83\% / 88\% strict), and Amprion is similarly
robust and balanced (84 / 80 / 86 under GEO). TenneT~DE matches nearly all of
its distinct plants (95\% GEO) but only 67\% of its volume---its largest
interventions slip through---with all three measures converging near 60\% under
the strict definition. 50Hertz is least matched at the plant level (30\% GEO,
13\% strict); because its event and volume shares stay far higher (82\% / 73\%
GEO), the unmatched plants are mostly small or infrequent while a few
well-matched large plants carry most of its redispatch energy.}
